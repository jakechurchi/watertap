All cases use a summer rate structure and full flexibility.
The DR refers to the Emergency Load Reduction program and CPP is the Critical Peak pricing
Note there is no DR baseline as it would be the same as the standard rate structure TOU3 without DR
The "lower_peak_baseline" refers to the demand response revenue being based on a lower power than the 1102 kW (steady-state baseline). 725 kW was selected as that is the power with the full flex, no DR case.
